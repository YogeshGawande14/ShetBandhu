# ShetBandhu

> Previously developed under the working title **AgroConnect**. The project has been renamed to **ShetBandhu** because AgroConnect is a commonly used name in the agriculture sector and may cause brand identity conflicts. The new name better represents the platform's mission of connecting farmers with fellow farmers and trusted local agricultural service centers across Maharashtra.

## About ShetBandhu

ShetBandhu is a Java 21 / Spring Boot 3.2 full-stack agricultural platform designed for Maharashtra's farming community.

The platform connects:

- Farmer ↔ Farmer
- Farmer ↔ Local Krishi Seva Kendra
- Farmer ↔ Local Agricultural Community

Unlike generic agricultural marketplaces, ShetBandhu focuses on creating a trusted local ecosystem where farmers can discover products, compare mandi rates, locate nearby agricultural service centers, estimate profits, and place orders through verified local businesses.

## Key Features

### Farmer-to-Farmer Network
Farmers can connect with and support other farmers within their local agricultural ecosystem.

### Local Krishi Seva Kendra Discovery
Search and discover verified nearby agricultural centers using area name or PIN code.

### Mandi Market Rates
View and compare mandi rates to make better selling decisions.

### Crop Profit Estimation
Calculate estimated profits based on market rates and transportation costs.

### Agricultural Marketplace
Browse agricultural products from trusted local vendors.

### Secure Authentication
JWT-based authentication with role-based access control.

### Order Management
Place and manage agricultural product orders with stock validation and status tracking.

### Marathi & English Support
Bilingual interface designed for Maharashtra farmers.

## Why ShetBandhu?

The name "ShetBandhu" reflects the vision of building a strong agricultural community where farmers help farmers and gain easy access to trusted local agricultural services.

Our goal is not only to create a marketplace but also to strengthen the farming ecosystem through collaboration, accessibility, and transparency.

### Vision

To become Maharashtra's most trusted digital platform connecting farmers, agricultural service providers, and local farming communities.

### Mission

Empower farmers through technology by simplifying access to agricultural products, services, market information, and community support.
